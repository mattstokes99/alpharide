Where AlphaRide Is Headed

A look at the real Alpharetta destinations and in-progress developments we're planning routes and pickup zones around — today's hotspots and what's still under construction.

Avalon

North Main Street
Live Now

Alpharetta's flagship 86-acre mixed-use district — 500,000+ sq ft of retail, dining, a 12-screen Regal Cinemas, and residences. Envisioned as a core pickup and drop-off hub.

Downtown Alpharetta

City Center
Live Now

City Hall, the Alpharetta Arts Center, and the "Around Lunch" dining district — the civic and social core of the city.

Halcyon

Forsyth Co. / North Alpharetta
Live Now

A $370M, 135-acre mixed-use development with restaurants, retail, offices, and homes — one of the largest developments in the area.

North Point

GA-400 Corridor
Live Now

Alpharetta's long-standing retail corridor along GA-400 — anchor shopping, big-box retail, and restaurants.

Ameris Bank Amphitheatre

Encore Park
Live Now

Metro Atlanta's premier outdoor concert venue — exactly the kind of event traffic an on-demand ride is built for.

Wills Park

Old Milton Parkway
Live Now

Alpharetta's recreation hub — equestrian center, aquatic center, sports fields, and one of the city's most-visited parks.

Northwinds Tech Corridor

Windward Pkwy / GA-400
Live Now

The office core of "Alpharetta, Technology City of the South" — a dense weekday commuter corridor well suited to short autonomous hops.

The Gathering

Haynes Bridge Road
Coming Soon

A Napa Valley-inspired open-air retail district less than a mile from downtown and Avalon. Vertical construction underway, with an anticipated opening in late 2026.

Northwinds Summit

Northwinds
Coming Soon

A large-scale project planned to deliver over 1.2 million sq ft of office space, a 150-room hotel, and retail/dining once complete.

Brookside Redevelopment

Old Milton area
Coming Soon

Portman Holdings' planned redevelopment of the Brookside office campus into a mixed-use district with retail, apartments, and townhomes.

Academy & Haynes Bridge

Near Downtown
Coming Soon

A planned mixed-use retail and dining project from Mayfair Street Partners, with a walkable design tying into the growing downtown district.

Locations shown reflect publicly reported Alpharetta destinations and in-progress developments as context for AlphaRide's route planning — not confirmed partnerships with these properties. Actual pickup zones, timing, and coverage will be finalized closer to launch and depend on Tesla's Cybercab availability.

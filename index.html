Why Cybercab

A New Way to Move Around Alpharetta

02

Fully Autonomous

Designed from the ground up for self-driving — Tesla has shown Cybercab prototypes with no steering wheel or pedals.

Built for Rides

A two-seat layout designed specifically for short, efficient point-to-point trips rather than long family road trips.

Electric & Quiet

Fully electric with inductive charging shown at Tesla's unveiling — no tailpipe, no gas station stops.

On-Demand

Tap a button, get picked up — the same app-based model Tesla has described for its Robotaxi network.

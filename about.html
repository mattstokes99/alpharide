Our Story

Why Alpharetta, Why Now

Alpharetta already looks like a city built for this: dense, walkable mixed-use hubs like Avalon and Halcyon sitting minutes apart, a fast-growing tech corridor along GA-400, and a downtown that keeps adding new places worth a short trip to. What's missing is an easy way to get between them without needing a car of your own for every stop.

AlphaRide is planning to fill that gap with a fleet built around Tesla's Cybercab — an autonomous, purpose-built ride-hailing vehicle. We don't control when Cybercab becomes available to operators like us; that's Tesla's timeline. What we control is being ready the moment it does: routes mapped, pickup zones planned, and a local waitlist ready to ride first.

A Note on Independence

We're Not Tesla — We're Getting Ready to Use Their Vehicle

AlphaRide is a separate, independent company. We have no formal partnership, license, or endorsement agreement with Tesla, Inc. "Cybercab" is Tesla's product name for the vehicle platform we plan to build our fleet around once it's commercially available to operators — the same way a taxi company might describe the make and model of car it drives, without being owned by that carmaker. Any specifications, pricing, or timelines referenced on this site come from Tesla's own public statements and are not guaranteed.
